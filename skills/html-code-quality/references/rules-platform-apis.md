# HTML Rules: Platform APIs

One directive per HTML rule in this area. Explicit local instructions prevail; see the [rule catalogue](../SKILL.md#rule-catalogue) for precedence notes.

- Never use elements obsolete in HTML5: replace `basefont`, `big`, `blink`, `center`, `font`, `marquee`, `multicol`, `nobr`, `spacer`, and `tt` with CSS; `acronym` with `abbr`; `applet` with `embed` or `object`; `bgsound` with `audio`; `dir` with `ul`; `hgroup` with `header` or `div`; `isindex` with form controls; `listing` with `pre` and `code`; `nextid` with GUIDs; `noembed` with `object` fallback content; `plaintext` with the `text/plain` MIME type; `strike` with `del` or `s`; and `xmp` with `pre` or `code`, escaping `<` and `&`; restructure pages that use `frame`, `frameset`, or `noframes`.
- Never use attributes obsolete in HTML5. Replace with CSS: `align`, `alink`, `allowtransparency`, `background`, `bgcolor`, `border` (except `border="0"` on `<img>`), `bordercolor`, `cellpadding`, `cellspacing`, `char`, `charoff`, `clear`, `color` on `<hr>`, `compact`, `frame` on `<table>`, `frameborder`, `height` on `<td>` and `<th>`, `hspace`, `link`, `marginbottom`, `marginheight`, `marginleft`, `marginright`, `margintop`, `marginwidth`, `noshade`, `nowrap`, `rules`, `scrolling`, `size` on `<hr>`, `text`, `type` on `<li>` and `<ul>`, `valign`, `vlink`, `vspace`, and `width` on `<col>`, `<hr>`, `<pre>`, `<table>`, `<td>`, and `<th>`. Move `accept` from `<form>` to each `<input>`; replace `archive`, `classid`, `code`, `codebase`, and `codetype` on `<object>` with `data` and `type`; `axis` with `scope` on the relevant `<th>`; `charset` on `<a>` and `<link>` with an HTTP `Content-Type` header; `coords` and `shape` on `<a>` with `<area>`; `datafld`, `dataformatas`, and `datasrc` with script that retrieves and populates content; `declare` by repeating the complete `<object>`; `event` and `for` on `<script>` with DOM event listeners; `language` on `<script>` with `type` (`language="javascript"` is permitted); `lowsrc` with a progressive JPEG; `methods` with HTTP `OPTIONS`; `name` on `<embed>`, `<img>`, and `<option>`, and on `<a>` unless it equals the element's `id`, with `id`; `scheme` on `<meta>` with one scheme per field or a scheme within the value; `scope` on `<td>` with `<th>`; `standby` with a resource that loads rapidly or incrementally; `summary` on `<table>` with a `<caption>`, a preceding `<p>`, or a `<figcaption>`; `urn` with `href`; `usemap` on `<input>` with `<img>`; and `type` and `valuetype` on `<param>` with the `data` attribute of `<object>`. Omit `datapagesize`, `ismap` on `<input>`, `nohref`, `profile`, `target` on `<link>`, and `version` on `<html>` altogether.
- Never use `<meta http-equiv="refresh">` to refresh or redirect a page; redirect with an HTTP 301 or 302 response, and refresh portions of the page by script.
- Never prevent zooming in the viewport `<meta>` element: no `user-scalable=no` and no `maximum-scale` of 2.0 or less, as WCAG 2.2 requires text resizable to 200% without assistive technology.
- Embed Flash content with an `<object>` element containing a nested `<embed>`, so that ActiveX browsers read the `<object>` and plug-in browsers read the `<embed>`.
